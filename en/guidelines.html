Video & Streaming Guidelines

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Anyone is welcome to make videos, streams and social posts with Pikomofu. You don't need to ask or let us know.

What you can do

  • Gameplay videos, live streams, shorts and screenshots
  • Let's plays, reactions, reviews and features. Say what you honestly think.
  • Monetize them (ad revenue, memberships, tips and so on)
  • Leave the app's sounds and music in
  • Use the app's screens and logo in titles and thumbnails
  • The same goes for individuals, companies and media

Please don't

  • Never use it, for any reason, in anything that harms animals or shows them being hurt
  • Present your video as official, or suggest a partnership with Pikomofu
  • Take the art, sounds or music out on their own for other works, asset packs or merchandise
  • Don't use it in content that hurts people, either

Nice to have

All optional.

  • A link to the App Store in your description
  • The hashtag #ピコモフ
  • Tell us about your video at @pikomofu_app on X or mofu@pikomofu.com. We'd love to watch it.

TV, radio and film

For broadcast or screenings, please write to mofu@pikomofu.com first; the music is licensed differently there. Footage and images for coverage are in the press kit.

These guidelines may change from time to time.
