Support

Caring for your pet

Use the Food, Water, Clean and Shower buttons under the garden. Its hearts for food, water and cleanliness go down slowly over time. It only eats or drinks when a heart is missing.

What the Pikomofu Pass adds

The Apple Watch app, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and up to five pets. A one-time purchase that works with Family Sharing. New friends and outfits arrive free with updates.

Restoring a purchase

Go to Settings (the gear) → Pikomofu Pass → Restore Purchase. Purchases made with the same Apple Account are restored.

Adding a widget

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen → Edit → Add Widget → Pikomofu (requires the Pass).

Using it on Apple Watch

Install Pikomofu from the Watch app on your iPhone (requires the Pass).

Weather

In Settings, turn on "Real weather in the garden" and choose a place (a prefecture in Japan, or a town anywhere in the world). Your iPhone's location is never used.

Turning the sound off

Use the speaker at the top right of the garden at any time. Sound effect and music volume are in Settings.

Sideways

Turn your iPhone sideways and the garden fills the screen. Turn off "Sideways: just the garden" in Settings to keep it upright.

Changing the app icon

Choose from 8 home screen icons in Settings → "App Icon". They are all free.

A new iPhone, or reinstalling

With "Keep in iCloud" on in Settings (it is on from the start), open the app on an iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account and the first screen offers "Pets kept in iCloud are waiting" — bring them home from there. Restoring an iPhone from a backup or moving to a new one with Quick Start also brings them along.

Moving with a file

In Settings → Moving, make a moving file, send it by AirDrop or email, and open it on the new iPhone. "Move everyone" brings the treasure box too; "Take some with you" brings only the ones you choose (they stay on the old iPhone as well). The Pikomofu Pass doesn't move with them, but on the same Apple Account the App Store gives it back.

Giving a pet to a new family

In Settings → Moving → Give to a new family, choose who goes. Once sent, they leave this iPhone and start again from "Day 1 together" in their new home (same name and look; the new family can rename them). The new family opens the file and taps "Welcome them". Up to five can live together; without the Pass, one is out in the garden and the others wait at home.

Deleting data

"Say goodbye to (name)" at the bottom of Settings removes that pet's records (this can't be undone). Deleting the app removes all data.

For parents

A few things worth knowing when your family uses Pikomofu.

  • Money: One pet is free forever. No ads, no loot boxes or gacha, no subscription. The only thing to buy is the Pikomofu Pass, a one-time purchase. With it, your pet is with you on Apple Watch, in widgets, on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and up to 5 pets can live together. “Support the maker” (tips) appears only for Pass owners, and tipping changes nothing in the app.
  • Purchases and limits: Every purchase goes through Apple, so Ask to Buy and Screen Time limits apply as usual. The Pass works with Family Sharing.
  • Data: No account, and no chat inside the app. The App Store privacy label says “Data Not Collected.” See the Privacy Policy for details.
  • Friends: A Playdate link and its QR code carry only the pet's name (up to 8 characters), its look and the day it came home. If a link is posted on social media, anyone who sees it can see that name and picture. “Visit a friend nearby” connects two iPhones only while both have that screen open: directly between them, encrypted, without the internet, and it sends only what a playdate link holds, plus the signals it needs to connect. All of these can simply be left unused.
  • Notifications: “Tell me when they want attention” is off at first. When it's on, it only says your pet wants some attention, and never between 10 PM and 7 AM.
  • Bond: Bond can only rise by a set amount each day, so playing longer gives no advantage. It never goes down, and no pet ever dies.
  • Text and age: In Japanese, the app's text is mostly hiragana. App Store age rating: 4+.
