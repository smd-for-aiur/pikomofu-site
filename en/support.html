Support

Caring for your pet

Use the Food, Water, Clean and Shower buttons under the garden. Its hearts for food, water and cleanliness go down slowly over time. It only eats or drinks when a heart is missing.

What the Pikomofu Pass adds

The Apple Watch app, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and up to five pets. A one-time purchase that works with Family Sharing. New friends and outfits arrive free with updates.

Restoring a purchase

Go to Settings (the gear) → Pikomofu Pass → Restore Purchase. Purchases made with the same Apple Account are restored.

Adding a widget

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen → Edit → Add Widget → Pikomofu (requires the Pass).

Using it on Apple Watch

Install Pikomofu from the Watch app on your iPhone (requires the Pass).

Weather

In Settings, turn on "Real weather in the garden" and choose a place (a prefecture in Japan, or a town anywhere in the world). Your iPhone's location is never used.

Turning the sound off

Use the speaker at the top right of the garden at any time. Sound effect and music volume are in Settings.

Sideways

Turn your iPhone sideways and the garden fills the screen. Turn off "Sideways: just the garden" in Settings to keep it upright.

Deleting data

"Say goodbye to (name)" at the bottom of Settings removes that pet's records (this can't be undone). Deleting the app removes all data.
