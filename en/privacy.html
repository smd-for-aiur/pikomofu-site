Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Pikomofu is an app made by smd. This page explains how it handles your information. Pikomofu is available for iPhone and Android. Where the Android version differs, it is noted with "On Android".

1. What we collect

Pikomofu does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, location or device identifiers. There is no account. It uses no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

2. What stays on your device

Your pets (names, looks, care records) and settings are stored only on your iPhone and your paired Apple Watch. They are never sent to the developer. Deleting the app removes them. (With "Keep in iCloud" on, it is also kept in iCloud, as below.) (On Android, they are kept on your Android device and, with Android's backup on, also in your Google Account, as below.)

3. iCloud and moving files

With "Keep in iCloud" on, your pets' data is also saved in the iCloud of your Apple Account. This is Apple's service, and the developer cannot see it (turning the setting off removes it). A moving file goes only where you choose to send it.

4. Android backup and moving files (Android)

On Android, your pets' data and settings are included in Android's standard backup (to your Google Account) and in transfers to a new device. This is Google's service, and the developer cannot see it. You can turn backup off in Android's settings. A moving file goes only where you choose to send it.

5. Weather (you can turn it on or off in Settings)

The approximate location (latitude and longitude) of the place you chose is sent through Pikomofu's weather relay (weather.pikomofu.com, running on Cloudflare) to Apple's weather service (WeatherKit) to get a forecast. The relay rounds the location to about 10 km before asking Apple and keeps no record (it reuses the answer for the same area for 30 minutes). If the relay can't be reached, your iPhone asks WeatherKit directly. Your iPhone's location is never used. When you search for a town outside Japan by name, what you type is sent to Apple Maps search. Apple's and Cloudflare's privacy policies also apply. On a device set to Japan (its region or time zone), it is on from the start with Tokyo as the place, so Tokyo's location is sent; you can change the place or turn it off in Settings. Elsewhere nothing is sent until you turn it on and choose a place. On Android, the weather always comes from Apple's weather service (WeatherKit) through Pikomofu's weather relay (weather.pikomofu.com, running on Cloudflare). The relay rounds the location to about 10 km before asking and keeps no record. When you search for a town outside Japan by name, what you type is sent to Android's place search (Google's geocoder), and Google's privacy policy applies. Your Android device's location is never used (the app doesn't ask for location permission).

6. Purchases

The Pikomofu Pass and tips are bought through Apple (the App Store). The developer never receives your payment information. On Android, the Pikomofu Pass and tips are bought through Google Play, and the developer never receives your payment details. The Pikomofu Pass works with the Google Account it was bought with (it can't be shared through Google Play Family Library).

7. Notifications

The "Tell me when they want attention" reminder is scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

8. Children

Because Pikomofu collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

9. This website

This website (pikomofu.com) counts page views with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It uses no cookies and does not track individual visitors. The app itself still collects nothing.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the change will be posted on this page.
